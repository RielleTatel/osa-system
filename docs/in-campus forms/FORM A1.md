## Reminders in the Conduct of Activities
- Make sure to turn off the lights/aircons/electric fans and faucets when not in use. Always CLAYGO.
- For outsiders, organizers must provide the USO the list of visitors and present any valid ID upon entering the campus.
- Keep event sounds at a moderate level.
- Keep emergency exits free at all times.
- For Sports Events, make sure that infirmary personnel (medic) is present at all times.
- For Food Services, avail of trusted catering services only.
- Provide separate rooms/quarters for sleeping (male and female).
- Moderator/s must stay with the participants for the entire duration of the activity (1 moderator for every 35 students).
- Approved itinerary must be strictly followed.
- Provide full details regarding mode of transportation.
